Centroid 3D Tours is run by Ben Stephens, a junior at Washington University in St. Louis studying finance, with a background in real estate development and urban design.

It started with listing photos that never matched what was actually behind the walls, and no dated record of what a unit looked like before move-in.

St. Louis has a lot of older housing stock, so wear and damage show up often and get expensive fast if they aren't documented.

Every shoot is done in person, and every report is personally reviewed before it goes out.
